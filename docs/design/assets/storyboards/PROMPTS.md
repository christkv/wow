# Storyboard Asset Provenance

All four sheets were generated on 2026-08-21 with the built-in OpenAI image-generation tool. They are exploratory pre-production images, not final game art. No source image or original game sprite was supplied as a reference. Written panel definitions in `../../03-storyboards.md` take precedence over visual ambiguities.

```text
Use case: illustration-story
Asset type: game-design storyboard sheet, SB-01 First Ninety Seconds
Primary request: a six-panel visual storyboard showing the first-time player experience for an original game concept inspired by early 1980s fixed-screen maze shooters: (1) ominous attract screen with a shadowy cosmic sorcerer looming over a neon dungeon, (2) player presses start and a yellow armored space adventurer waits in a protected launch alcove, (3) adventurer enters a glowing single-screen maze while six blue creatures roam, (4) a clean laser shot destroys the first creature, (5) one blue creature transforms into a faster yellow hunter while a radar strip signals danger, (6) the player and a blue AI companion stand back-to-back at a corridor junction.
Scene/backdrop: black void around a single luminous dungeon; thin electric-blue maze walls; lower radar strip.
Subject: readable actions and changing game state, one yellow adventurer, one blue companion, blue and yellow monsters.
Style/medium: loose production storyboard art combining crisp pixel-art silhouettes with hand-drawn cinematic annotations; original designs, not copied sprites.
Composition/framing: landscape 3-by-2 grid, six equal panels, strong panel gutters, consistent top-down three-quarter game view.
Lighting/mood: fluorescent cyan, cobalt, yellow, and red on black; eerie but playful retro-arcade tension.
Text: panel numerals "1", "2", "3", "4", "5", "6" only.
Constraints: each panel must communicate one beat at thumbnail size; same character designs across all panels; no logos, no game title, no dialogue, no extra captions, no trademarks, no watermark.
Avoid: modern military realism, detailed UI text, photorealism, side-scrolling views.
```
