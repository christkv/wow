# Music source map

All three music sources are offered under CC0 1.0 by their respective authors on OpenGameArt. Attribution is not required by CC0, but it is preserved here as a production courtesy.

| Production role | Runtime file(s) | Work and author | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Attract screen | `attract-intro.ogg`, `attract-loop.ogg` | “8-Bit Space Adventure Theme” by emanresU | <https://opengameart.org/content/8-bit-space-adventure-theme> |
| Standard dungeon | `dungeon-loop.mp3`, `dungeon-loop.ogg` | “8-bit Epic Space Shooter Music” by HydroGene | <https://opengameart.org/content/8-bit-epic-space-shooter-music> |
| Gaoler / Pit | `gaoler-loop.ogg`, `pit-climax-loop.ogg` | “8bit Action Boss Battle” by MintoDog | <https://opengameart.org/content/8bit-action-boss-battle> |

The OGG version of `dungeon-loop` is an audio-format conversion of the downloaded MP3. The other runtime files are renamed copies of the source OGG files. No composition has been resampled into another work.
